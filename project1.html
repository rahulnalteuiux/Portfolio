SecureLink

Redesigning a cross-platform communication system for secure defence operations, enabling reliable offline data exchange between Windows and RHEL systems while improving usability, visibility, and workflow efficiency.

This case study omits sensitive product, operational, technical, deployment details.

Visuals and descriptions are presented at a high level to protect confidential project information.

Where project evidence was unavailable, assumptions and qualitative observations are clearly identified.

Problem
Cross-platform made offline data exchange difficult and unclear.
Solution

Impact

Learning

Technical configuration made starting a transfer more difficult than necessary.

02

Limited Visibility

Users had limited visibility into connection, transfer, and system status.

03

Unclear Recovery

Technical errors did not clearly communicate what happened or what to do next.

The Final Look

Outcome & Takeaways

What Changed & What I Learned

Key Takeaways

Complexity Needs Clarity

Simplify technical workflows without hiding important information.

Status Builds Confidence

Clear connection and transfer feedback helps users stay confident.

Recovery Needs Direction

Errors should explain what happened and guide users toward the next step.

Reflection

"This project taught me that in secure environments, good UX is about making complex communication clear, visible, and predictable—without taking control away from the user."
