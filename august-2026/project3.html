1: Enterprise Dashboard

Establishing a high-level command view.

Rather than technical jargon, the interface used actionable connection cards displaying Adapter Name, IP, Speed, and Cable Status.

  • 🟢 Connected
  • 🟡 Pending
  • 🔴 Disconnected

2: Smart Empty States & Error Prevention

Ensuring metadata completeness.

Generic technical exceptions like "Socket Exception" or "No Adapter Found" were replaced with actionable steps to assist non-networkers.

3: Immutable Activity Tracking

Building organizational accountability.

To prevent repeated button clicking out of uncertainty, the transfer UI included a dedicated drag-and-drop zone, live progress bars, speed metrics, and estimated time remaining.
